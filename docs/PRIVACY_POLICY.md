# FlowCycle — Health Data Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: August 20, 2026  
**Last Updated**: August 20, 2026  

At **FlowCycle**, we believe your reproductive health data is among the most sensitive personal information in existence. Our privacy philosophy is simple: **Your health data belongs to you, stays on your device, and is never sold, monetized, or brokered.**

---

## 1. Core Privacy Commitments

1. **Zero Data Selling**: We will never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal health, menstrual, or fertility information to third-party data brokers, insurers, or advertising networks.
2. **Offline-First Storage**: By default, all cycle predictions, symptom logs, intimate health entries, and notes are encrypted and stored locally on your device.
3. **No Hidden Trackers**: FlowCycle does not include invasive analytics or advertising trackers that trace your identity across apps or websites.
4. **Client-Side De-Identification**: When utilizing AI companion features, all personally identifiable information (names, emails, dates of birth) is permanently scrubbed on your device before queries are submitted.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect and Where It Lives

### A. Local Device Data (Never automatically transmitted to servers)
* **Cycle Logs**: Menstrual period dates, flow intensity, pain scores, and cycle regularity history.
* **Biomarkers & Fertility Data**: Basal Body Temperature (BBT), LH/OPK test results, cervical mucus textures, and intercourse logs.
* **Daily Well-being Entries**: Mood, physical symptoms, cravings, sleep duration, and water intake.
* **App Security Settings**: Custom 4-digit PIN lock and biometric preferences.

### B. Optional Account Data (If using cloud backup or Social Sign-In)
* **Authentication Credentials**: Name and email address when creating an optional account via Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In (including Apple Private Relay), or Email.
* **Sync State**: Encrypted backup data if you choose to enable cloud backup.

---

## 3. Artificial Intelligence & Gemini AI Privacy Architecture

FlowCycle includes an optional AI Companion powered by Google Gemini and a 100% offline clinical intelligence engine:

* **Offline Clinical Engine (Default)**: Generates phase-based health tips, cycle breakdowns, and educational material 100% locally on your device with zero internet transmission.
* **Cloud AI Requests (Optional)**: When asking interactive questions to cloud AI:
  1. Requests are stripped of all PII (names, emails, exact locations, identifiers) client-side.
  2. Data sent to the AI service is ephemeral and strictly used to generate the immediate clinical response.
  3. Your conversation history is stored locally in your private on-device database.

---

## 4. Security Measures

* **Local App Lock**: You can secure FlowCycle with a custom 4-digit PIN or device biometrics (Face ID / Fingerprint).
* **Doctor Export (PDF/CSV)**: You retain 100% ownership of your records. You can export a comprehensive clinical report to share directly with your healthcare provider at any time.

---

## 5. Your Data Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Compliance)

Regardless of your geographic location, FlowCycle provides you with complete data autonomy:
* **Right to Access**: View all stored cycle history and daily entries at any time in the app.
* **Right to Export**: Download your complete dataset in standard JSON, CSV, or PDF format.
* **Right to Delete**: Purge all local and cloud data permanently in **Settings ➔ Privacy & Data ➔ Delete Data**.
