# FlowCycle Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 27, 2026**

FlowCycle (“FlowCycle,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, when it may be shared, and the choices you have regarding your information when you use the FlowCycle mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

Because FlowCycle allows you to record information about your menstrual cycle, fertility, symptoms, sexual activity, wellness, and other personal observations, we treat this information as sensitive and handle it with particular care.

By using FlowCycle, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, FlowCycle may collect the following categories of information:

### 1.1 Account Information
When you create or maintain an account, we may collect:
- Name or display name
- Email address
- Password or authentication information
- Profile photograph, if provided
- Account creation date
- Subscription or membership status
- Account preferences

### 1.2 Cycle and Fertility Information
If you choose to use FlowCycle’s tracking features, we may collect:
- Period start and end dates
- Cycle length & period length
- Cycle history
- Ovulation information & fertile-window observations
- Menstrual flow intensity
- Symptoms and physical observations
- Pregnancy-related information you choose to provide
- Trying-to-conceive or cycle-awareness mode preferences

*Some of this information may constitute sensitive or specially protected personal information under applicable law.*

### 1.3 Health and Wellness Information (Optional)
You may voluntarily provide information such as:
- Mood and emotional state
- Sleep duration and quality
- Exercise and workouts
- Lifestyle observations & journal entries
- Basal body temperature (BBT)
- Other custom wellness notes

You are not required to provide optional information to use the core Service.

### 1.4 Sexual and Relationship Information (Optional)
Certain FlowCycle features allow you to record information concerning sexual activity, intimacy, libido, or related observations. You decide whether to provide this information.

### 1.5 Device and Technical Information
We may automatically receive limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the Service, such as:
- Device type and operating system
- App version, language, and region
- Crash logs and diagnostic telemetry
- IP address or approximate location where required for security or technical verification
- Authentication session tokens

### 1.6 Usage Information
We may collect information about how you interact with FlowCycle (screens accessed, settings selected). Where possible, analytics information is minimized, aggregated, and separated from sensitive health records.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use information solely to:
- Provide and operate FlowCycle
- Create and maintain your account
- Store your cycle history and generate cycle calculations
- Provide fertile-window, cycle-phase, and ovulation estimates
- Provide personalized insights and enabled notifications
- Improve the accuracy and usability of the Service
- Provide customer support and process subscriptions
- Secure accounts, prevent fraud, and maintain encrypted backups
- Comply with legal obligations

We will **never** use sensitive information for purposes unrelated to the operation, security, personalization, or improvement of FlowCycle.

---

## 3. Personalization and Predictions

FlowCycle uses information you provide to calculate estimates and generate insights (estimated cycle lengths, period dates, fertile windows, and ovulation timing). 

*These are mathematical estimates generated from the information available and are not guaranteed to be clinically exact.*

---

## 4. AI Companion

FlowCycle provides an AI Companion allowing you to ask questions and receive educational responses. 
- Information shared with the AI Companion is anonymized.
- Personal health conversations are **never used to train public external LLM models**.
- The AI Companion is not a doctor, specialist, or therapist, and must not be used for diagnosis or emergency medical care.

---

## 5. How We Share Information

**We do NOT sell your personal health information.**

We disclose information only in limited circumstances:
- **Service Providers**: Trusted infrastructure partners (cloud hosting, database infrastructure, payment processing, error monitoring, notification delivery) bound by strict confidentiality.
- **Legal Requirements**: Where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect user safety, or enforce our Terms.
- **Business Transfers**: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or restructuring, subject to applicable legal protections.

---

## 6. Analytics and Advertising

We will **never** provide advertisers with your private cycle history, fertility records, sexual-health records, or journal entries for targeted advertising.

---

## 7. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards:
- **AES-256 Encryption** for sensitive health data at rest.
- **TLS 1.3 Encryption** for data in transit.
- **Device-First Storage** and in-app **PIN / Biometric Locks**.

---

## 8. Data Retention & Erasure

We retain information as long as necessary to provide the Service. You may request permanent deletion of your account and health records at any time directly through the app or by contacting us.

---

## 9. Your Privacy Choices (GDPR & CCPA Rights)

Depending on your jurisdiction, your rights include:
- Accessing and reviewing your personal information
- Requesting data export (PDF Clinical Reports or CSV format)
- Requesting instant erasure and account deletion
- Managing notifications, permissions, and AI sharing toggles
